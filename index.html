Our Story

Harnav Ventures — The Foundation for Inspired Spaces

Harnav Ventures is a Jaipur-based supplier of high-quality plywood, MDF boards, particle board, laminates and ceiling materials. We work with builders, contractors, interior designers, retailers and homeowners, providing genuine products, competitive pricing and reliable delivery across Rajasthan.

We believe that the materials that go into a space define how it feels and how long it lasts. That's why every product we supply is graded, moisture-tested and dispatched from our own yard — so you get what is stated on the invoice, every time.

From residential homes to large commercial fit-outs, our team is committed to making material procurement simple, transparent and dependable.

Why Choose Harnav Ventures?

Panels are cheap to buy and expensive to live with. Here is what we do differently.

01
📋
Grade Stated on Every Invoice
BWR, BWP or MR — you get the certificate with the load, not a promise that it is 'the good one'. Full transparency on every delivery.
02
💧
Dry Stock, Checked Before Dispatch
Every batch is moisture-tested and conditioned. Warping after installation is our problem, not yours. We stand behind what we supply.
03
📦
Bundled to Your Cut List
Send us your shutter and wardrobe schedules and we will break the bundles down to the sizes you actually install. Less waste for you.
04
🏭
One Yard, Seven Product Lines
Boards, laminates and ceiling systems sourced together, so a full fit-out does not run on three deliveries from three different suppliers.
05
🤝

06

Reliable Delivery Across Rajasthan
